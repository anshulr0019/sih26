# God's Eye View reference

The Cesium provider approach in `src/components/GlobeCanvas.js` is adapted from
[God's Eye View](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view), inspected at
commit `a547e99a7b9f74acadef4a89bcd1377405cb750c`.
The dashboard integration and theme are specific to this project. No bundled
datasets, 3D models, promotional images, or live flight/ship/voice modules were imported.
